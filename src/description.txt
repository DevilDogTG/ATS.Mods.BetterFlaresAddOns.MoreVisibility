[orange]DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (7500K LED)

[normal]An American Truck Simulator add-on for Better Flares v4.9 - Headlights.2 7500K LED. Your truck's
headlights spread wider and the low and high beams reach farther for more comfortable night driving, and the
headlamp glow stays readable in bad daytime conditions (rain, fog, overcast) - while keeping Better Flares'
realistic mood. The ATS twin of the ETS2 mod of the same name (2.1.0).
--------------------
[orange]What changes
[normal]- Wider beams: every truck headlight stage (low, high, front and roof lamps) spreads 103
  degrees instead of 90, so road shoulders and curves are better lit.
- Longer, brighter low beam: reaches 220 instead of 140 and is 30% brighter, so the road stays
  visible at dusk. The cutoff line is sharper (the glow above it fades out faster), and its left
  side is raised to the right side's level, so the left of the road is lit farther ahead.
- Longer high beam: reaches 320 instead of 240; the front lamps reach 350 instead of 260.
- More visible headlamps: the glow of the lamp itself is a little larger and stays visible from
  wider angles, so trucks read better in poor daylight.

What does NOT change:
- High beam and aux lamp brightness, roof lamp reach, and the light just ahead of the bumper: Better Flares
  v4.9's own values are kept.
- Beam aim and beam pattern: your Better Flares Headlights.1 choice (any of them) is kept;
  only its low-beam cutoff is sharpened and its left side raised.
- Light color temperature (7500K LED).
- Tail lights, indicators, side markers and all other lights.

Works on every ATS 1.62 truck: Better Flares Headlights.1 covers all 19.
--------------------
[orange]Requirements / load order
[normal]Top = highest priority in Mod Manager:
1. DriveDogs: Better Flares City Lights (optional)
2. DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (7500K LED)   <- this mod
3. Better Flares - Traffic Addon (ATS) (optional)
4. Better Flares - Headlights.2 7500K LED
5. Better Flares - Headlights.1 (any one)
6. Better Flares - City Lights Addon (optional)
7. Better Flares - Base

Only for Better Flares v4.9 and American Truck Simulator 1.62. Headlights.1 and Headlights.2 are both
required. This mod replaces Headlights.2's beam values, so loaded above another Headlights.2 colour it
still gives 7500K light. The headlamp glow change may also apply to other vehicles that use Better
Flares' headlight glow, such as AI traffic.

Better Flares is by Avelium. The sharpened low-beam masks are modified Better Flares textures.
